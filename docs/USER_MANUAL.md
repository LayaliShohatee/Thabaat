# Thabaat user manual (backlog #155)

For members and guests using the Thabaat app.

## Getting started
- **Create an account:** open Thabaat, tap **Create one**, enter your name, email and a password.
- **Browse without an account:** on the sign-in screen tap **Browse public events without signing in**.
- **Profile:** Profile → Edit Profile. Gender and birth year are optional; organizers only see them as attendee totals and filters.
- **Forgot password:** Sign in → **Forgot your password?** and follow the email link.

## Finding events
- **Home** lists upcoming events. Use search and the **When** (Next 7 days / Next month) and **Venue** filters.
- Open an event for details, speaker, age or gender eligibility, logistics and FAQs.
- **Add to calendar:** Device Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.
- **Share:** use **Share event link**. Friends without the app see the public event page.

## Registering and tickets
1. Open an event → **Register for Event**.
2. Choose a ticket type (for example Adult, Child, VIP or Early bird). Early bird tickets disappear when their sale ends.
3. For paid tickets, enter a discount code and tap **Check my price**. Members see their member discount automatically.
4. Tap **Confirm Registration**. After a free registration you can make an optional suggested donation.
5. Your ticket appears in **My Events** → **Open ticket**. Show the QR code at check-in.

## Changing plans
- **Cancel a free ticket:** My Events → **Cancel free registration**.
- **Paid ticket refund:** My Events → **Cancel and request refund** (before the event's refund deadline). The event team reviews it.
- **Transfer a ticket:** open the ticket → **Transfer this ticket to someone else**. They have 24 hours to accept.

## Membership
- Profile → **Membership** shows your plan, renewal date and benefits.
- **Join:** choose a plan to open the secure CharityStack checkout.
- **Stop or keep renewing / switch plans:** use **Manage your membership**. Changes apply at your next billing date.

## Donations
Profile → **Support Thabaat** lists campaigns with progress bars. Donations open securely in CharityStack.

## Notifications
Profile → **Notification Preferences**: turn push on or off, choose reminders (1 week, 24 hours, morning of, starting now), categories and video alerts.

## Videos (members)
Profile → **Media library**: search, filter by category, play, resume where you left off, keep listening in the background or watch in picture-in-picture.

## Volunteering
Apply in **Permissions**. Once approved, complete the onboarding checklist in **My volunteer shifts**, then use arrive/depart for each shift. You get a reminder 24 hours before each shift.
